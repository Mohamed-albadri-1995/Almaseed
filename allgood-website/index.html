About us

Rooted in the Middle East, headquartered in Saudi Arabia

ALL GOOD Power Construction is an industrial trading and engineering service group. We work across five sectors (industrial, petrochemical, power, construction and communications) and provide one-stop solutions for large international energy and engineering projects.

We supply industrial materials, deliver construction services on site, and lease and maintain heavy equipment, with our own transport fleet and hubs in Riyadh, Dammam and Jeddah.

Mission

To build long-term partnerships with world-class EPC contractors through strong products and professional service, supporting Saudi Arabia's energy and infrastructure development.

Vision

To be the most trusted industrial trading and engineering service group in the Middle East, and to take an active part in Saudi Vision 2030.

What we do

Five business units, one point of contact

01

Electrical materials supply

Exclusive authorized agent of Bahra Electric, Saudi Arabia's largest local cable manufacturer. SEC-compliant.

  • Medium & low voltage power cables
  • Control & instrumentation cables
  • Fiber optic composite cables
  • Cable tray systems
  • Distribution boxes, transformers, generators
  • Communications accessories & fiber optic cabinets
02

Steel structure & metal fabrication

In-house manufacturing for oil, chemical and power infrastructure. Built to ASME, ASTM, DIN and GB standards.

  • Pressure pipe systems
  • Industrial valves
  • High-strength fasteners
  • Embedded parts & anchor bolts
  • Steel structure components
03

Engineering construction services

Professional crews working to international standards, with a zero safety incident record.

  • Waterproofing
  • Anti-corrosion & insulation
  • Scaffolding
  • Industrial pipeline installation
  • Temporary camp construction
04

Generators & heavy equipment

Agency brands: Cummins diesel generator sets, Stamford alternators and Deep Sea controllers. Installation, commissioning and maintenance across the Kingdom.

  • Truck cranes (multiple tonnages)
  • Aerial work platforms
  • Heavy-haul trailer transport
  • 24-hour emergency response
05

Cable tray factory Under development

A fully automated local factory, in line with Saudi Vision 2030 localization and IKTVA local content goals. Together with Bahra Electric, it will offer a complete cable-and-tray package from one supplier.

Tray types

  • Trough
  • Perforated
  • Ladder
  • Wide-span

Materials

  • Hot-dip galvanized steel
